Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Jan 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Cricket Ball Speed Tracking App (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

a. Video Data

The App allows users to record or upload cricket bowling videos for speed estimation. Videos are used only for processing and are not shared.

b. Device Information

Limited device information such as model and OS version may be collected to improve performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • Analyze video frames
  • Improve accuracy
  • Fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Videos may be processed locally or temporarily stored and are deleted after processing.

4. Data Sharing

No personal data is shared with third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13.
